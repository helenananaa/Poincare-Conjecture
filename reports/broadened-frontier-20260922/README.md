## Three parallel branches

The topology branch constructs nested buffered coordinate-cell presentations,
actual collapse maps with controlled fibers, punctured-sphere coordinates, and
then the open exterior of a coordinate closed ball in a known sphere. The
relative-collapse theorem additionally preserves the complement of a prescribed
neighborhood. An open exterior homeomorphic to R3 is NOT the desired marked
closed complement homeomorphic to the closed ball; sum_sphere stays unbound.

The metric-coefficient branch derives uniform coercivity on compact parameter
sets, constructs actual inverses, controls perturbations and inverse differences,
and develops the six-component heat and positivity-preserving averaging steps.
None of this constructs a global metric-valued Ricci evolution.

The heat branch isolates common domination, first-derivative and gradient-
derivative exchanges, and Hessian continuity. Their reducer is the exact old
truncated-C2 endpoint. Uniform value/derivative limits and a two-derivative limit
lemma are separate inputs to a later full, untruncated Duhamel C2 parent.

## Classical route review (not a formalization claim)

Reference: Morton Brown, A proof of the generalized Schoenflies theorem,
Bulletin of the AMS 66 (1960), pp. 74–76, especially Theorems 1–5.
